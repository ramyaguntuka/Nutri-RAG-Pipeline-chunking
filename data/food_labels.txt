# Food Labels

## Read the ingredient list
Read the complete ingredient list, not only the front-of-pack claims. Check for ingredients that conflict with allergies or dietary choices. In packaged foods, formulations can change, so check the label each time.

## Compare products
Compare similar products using the nutrition information and serving sizes shown on their labels. A health claim on the front does not replace checking ingredients and nutrition details.

## Limits of labels
Label formats and allergen declarations vary by country. If an ingredient or allergen status is unclear, contact the manufacturer or choose a product with clear information.
