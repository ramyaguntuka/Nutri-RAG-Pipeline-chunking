# Balanced Plate

## What makes a balanced plate
A balanced plate can include a variety of vegetables and fruits, grains or other staple foods, and protein-rich foods. The right combination depends on a person's preferences, culture, needs, and access to food. Include a variety of colors and food groups over the day rather than expecting every meal to be perfect.

## Practical portions
Use the plate as a flexible visual guide, not a medical prescription or a strict calorie target. Add vegetables where available, choose whole grains when practical, and include a protein source such as beans, lentils, eggs, dairy, fish, or meat according to the person's diet. Drink water with meals if desired.

## Keep it realistic
Frozen, canned, and seasonal produce can be practical options. A balanced eating pattern is built over time. This note does not set personal calorie needs or treat health conditions.
