# Healthy Cooking

## Everyday cooking methods
Boiling, steaming, baking, grilling, and sauteing are practical cooking methods. Use herbs, spices, citrus, or aromatics to add flavor. Cooking choices should fit the food, equipment, and preferences.

## Build a meal
Combine a staple such as rice or another grain with beans, lentils, eggs, tofu, dairy, fish, or meat according to the diet, plus vegetables when available. Use a variety of ingredients across the week.

## Safe preparation
Keep raw and ready-to-eat foods separate and follow safe food-storage instructions. Adapt recipes for allergies and check packaged ingredients.
