# Food Allergies

## Allergy awareness
Food allergies can cause serious reactions. Avoid foods and ingredients that a person has identified as allergens, and read ingredient labels every time because recipes and manufacturing can change.

## Cross-contact
Food can come into contact with an allergen through shared equipment, surfaces, utensils, or cooking oil. Ask about preparation and cross-contact when eating away from home. If ingredient information is unclear, do not assume a food is safe.

## Getting help
This guide cannot assess an individual's allergy risk or treat a reaction. For personal allergy advice, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For a suspected severe or rapidly worsening reaction, seek emergency help immediately.
