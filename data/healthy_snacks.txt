# Healthy Snacks

## Snack ideas
Practical snack ideas include seasonal fruit, roasted chana, plain yogurt, or vegetables with a suitable dip. Select options that fit the person's diet and allergies. These are examples rather than a required meal schedule.

## Keep snacks convenient
Wash and portion fruit or vegetables ahead of time when possible. Keep shelf-stable choices such as roasted chana available if they suit your dietary needs and allergy profile.

## Choose mindfully
Packaged snacks differ in ingredients and nutrition. Check labels for allergens and compare serving sizes. Local prices and availability vary.
