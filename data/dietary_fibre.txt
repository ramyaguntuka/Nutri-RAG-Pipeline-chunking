# Dietary Fibre

## Fibre-containing foods
Beans, lentils, peas, vegetables, fruits, oats, and whole-grain foods are sources of dietary fibre. Include a range of these foods in meals and snacks when they fit your diet and are available.

## Increasing fibre gradually
If you want to eat more fibre, add fibre-containing foods gradually and drink fluids regularly. Sudden large changes may be uncomfortable for some people. Individual needs differ, and this note does not provide treatment advice for digestive symptoms.

## Everyday swaps
Try beans or lentils in a dish, choose oats or a whole-grain option when practical, and include whole fruit rather than relying only on juice. Check labels and ingredient lists for allergens.
