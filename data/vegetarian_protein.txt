# Vegetarian Protein

## Protein food examples
Vegetarian protein foods include lentils, beans, chickpeas, peas, tofu, tempeh, nuts, seeds, eggs, and dairy products. Vegan choices exclude eggs and dairy; check that each suggested food fits the person's diet and allergies.

## Add protein to meals
Include a protein-rich food in meals, such as dal with rice, beans with a grain, tofu with vegetables, or yogurt with oats when dairy is suitable. Variety across meals can make vegetarian eating more flexible.

## Practical choices
Dried or canned legumes, tofu, eggs, and dairy may be convenient depending on local access and budget. Check ingredient labels for allergens and do not assume a plant-based product is allergen-free.
