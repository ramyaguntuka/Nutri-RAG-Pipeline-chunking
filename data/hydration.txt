# Hydration

## Everyday fluids
Water is a simple drink choice. Fluid needs vary with weather, activity, age, health, and other individual factors, so one amount is not right for everyone.

## Helpful habits
Keep water available and drink regularly according to thirst and personal circumstances. Foods such as fruits and vegetables also contain water, though they do not replace individualized medical guidance.

## When to seek advice
This general note cannot determine someone's hydration needs or diagnose dehydration. Ask a qualified healthcare professional for advice about a health condition or fluid restriction.
