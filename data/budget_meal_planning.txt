# Budget Meal Planning

## Planning with a budget
Plan a few meals before shopping, check what is already at home, and compare unit prices. Dried beans and lentils, eggs, grains, and seasonal or frozen vegetables can be useful staples where available. Choose foods that fit allergies and the household's diet.

## Reduce food waste
Use leftovers safely in another meal, store food according to package guidance, and freeze portions when suitable. Preparing a short shopping list can help avoid impulse purchases.

## Flexible meals
Build a meal around an affordable staple, a protein food, and vegetables or fruit when available. Local prices and access differ; this guide does not promise a specific daily meal cost.
