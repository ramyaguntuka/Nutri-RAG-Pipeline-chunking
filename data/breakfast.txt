# Breakfast

## Simple breakfast ideas
Breakfast can combine a filling grain or staple with a protein food and fruit or vegetables. Examples include oats with milk or a suitable fortified alternative and fruit; eggs with whole-grain toast and vegetables; or idli with sambar. Choose options that fit the person's diet and available ingredients.

## Prepare ahead
Overnight oats, boiled eggs, or pre-cut fruit can save time. Check packaged foods for allergens and ingredients that do not fit your diet. Breakfast is optional; appetite and routines differ among people.

## Budget-friendly choices
Oats, eggs, seasonal fruit, and locally available staples can make practical breakfasts. Prices vary by location and season, so compare local options rather than relying on a fixed price.
